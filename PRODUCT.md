# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operations coordinators working from a desktop control center, monitoring a fleet of trucks across active routes — including international crossings. They manage many trips simultaneously, often under time pressure, and need to triage status quickly without hunting for information. Secondary users include logistics managers (oversight, reporting) and drivers (low-friction mobile confirmations).

## Product Purpose

IASA is a logistics operational visibility system. It replaces manual, fragmented tracking — phone calls, WhatsApp messages, spreadsheet reconstruction — with a single platform that surfaces the real state of every trip in real time.

The goal is not to show trucks on a map. The goal is to answer: what is happening, why is it happening, how long has it been happening, and what should be done. Success means coordinators spend less time chasing status and more time acting on it. The operation transforms from reactive and fragmented to observable, anticipable, and coordinated.

Core surfaces: unified operational dashboard (fleet overview, active trips, incidents, alerts), trip detail view (timeline, milestones, cargo, vehicle, driver, carrier, incidents), incident management, analytics, driver mobile confirmation flow.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, reliable. The system should feel like a trusted instrument — authoritative but never loud. It earns confidence through clarity and consistency, not through visual noise or decoration. Think: a well-calibrated control room, not a consumer app.

Three words: **preciso, confiable, sereno**.

## Anti-references

- Generic enterprise SaaS dashboards: blue-heavy, card-grid layouts, hero metric templates (big number + small label + gradient accent), identical stat counters. Most logistics incumbents fall here.
- Consumer or playful product design: bright colors for decoration, rounded bubbly components, onboarding illustration style. This is operational software used under pressure.
- Any aesthetic the coordinator could mistake for a demo or prototype.

## Design Principles

1. **Contexto sobre coordenadas.** State, reason, and elapsed time matter more than a GPS pin. Every data point should answer an operational question, not just display a value.
2. **Claridad bajo presión.** Coordinators triage multiple trips at once. Critical states must be immediately scannable — color, hierarchy, and density must work together to surface what needs attention first.
3. **Autoridad tranquila.** Confidence through restraint. The interface should feel like a reliable instrument: precise, consistent, never noisy. Decoration that adds no information is removed.
4. **Fricción mínima, información máxima.** Every interaction — from confirming a milestone to logging an incident — should be as short as possible. The system does the work; the user confirms or acts.
5. **Observable sin abrumar.** Information density is intentional. Surface what matters at each level of zoom (fleet → trip → event), not everything at once.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. The interface is used in Spanish only. Status distinctions (trip states, incident severity, alert levels) must never rely on color alone — always pair with iconography or label. Reduced motion support required: coordinators may use the app for long sessions, and excessive animation adds fatigue. Consider users on lower-resolution monitors in office environments.
